Audit of existing site

What's working — and what isn't

Warnings 6 items

Only 2 pages crawled
this is critically low and suggests either a very thin site, crawl-blocking (robots.txt, JS rendering, or noindex tags), or a site that is barely launched; the redesign brief must confirm actual page count before IA planning
Phone number is missing from all scraped data
this may indicate the number is rendered in an image, loaded via JavaScript after page load, or stored only in a third-party widget; all three scenarios harm local SEO, accessibility, and click-to-call conversion
No images found across the site
combined with only 2 crawled pages, this raises the possibility the current site is a placeholder or severely underdeveloped, which means the redesign is effectively a ground-up build rather than a refresh
No testimonials present
in a technology-forward practice where patients may be skeptical of unfamiliar workflows, the absence of social proof is a conversion risk that should be treated as a launch-blocker for the new site
City/state for Mansfield is unconfirmed
there are multiple Mansfields (TX, OH, MA, CT); the correct market must be confirmed before any local keyword targeting, schema markup, or citation-building work begins
Social profiles found (2) but not identified by platform
these should be audited to confirm they are active, consistently branded as 'Mansfield Digital Dentistry', and linked bidirectionally from the new site for local SEO co-citation

Content gaps 7 items

Zero patient testimonials or reviews surfaced
this is a critical trust gap for a practice asking patients to invest in technology-forward care; Google review schema and on-site testimonial blocks must be added
No images found across crawled pages
a digital dentistry practice with no visual content of the technology, office, or doctor completely undercuts the brand promise; before/after galleries, equipment photography, and a doctor headshot are essential
No phone number detected
the most basic conversion element is missing or unscrapable, suggesting it may not be crawlable text (e.g., image-based or JavaScript-rendered), which is an accessibility and SEO failure
Service pages for high-value restorative treatments absent from the detected list
Dental Implants, Porcelain Veneers, Invisalign/Clear Aligners, and Root Canal Therapy are not present despite being top-searched services in any dental market; their absence is a significant organic traffic gap
No hub pages planned for major service categories
without Restorative, Cosmetic, and Digital Technology hub pages, the site will have orphaned individual service pages and weak internal linking architecture
No sedation dentistry page
given the technology positioning and likely anxious-patient audience, sedation options (even nitrous) should be addressed explicitly
Doctor bio exists but its depth is unknown
needs to include dental school, years in practice, technology training/certifications, and any continuing education specifics to support E-E-A-T

SEO opportunities 7 items

Target 'digital dentistry Mansfield' and 'same-day crowns Mansfield'
the practice name directly matches high-intent local queries that competitors with generic names cannot easily win
Build a Digital Dentistry hub page optimized for 'digital dental technology [city]' that links down to Digital Diagnostics, Crowns (same-day/CEREC angle), and Fillings
this creates a crawlable technology pillar with internal link equity flow
Emergency dentistry page should target 'emergency dentist Mansfield' and 'same-day dentist Mansfield'
these are high-CPC, high-intent queries; the page needs a prominent phone CTA and hours information that are currently missing
Implement LocalBusiness + Dentist schema with sameAs links to the two detected social profiles, and DentistPractice structured data for Dr. Lee
this directly supports the Google local knowledge panel given the missing phone number issue
FAQ content already exists
wrap existing FAQs in FAQPage schema markup to capture featured snippet real estate for question-based queries like 'what is digital dentistry' and 'how long do digital crowns take'
The practice phone number must be rendered as crawlable HTML text (not an image or JS-only element) sitewide
this is both an SEO and a Core Web Vitals / accessibility issue that blocks click-to-call tracking and local citation consistency
If a third-party booking tool is used, ensure an on-domain /contact or /schedule page exists as a canonical scheduling entry point
orphaning all booking flow to an external domain loses link equity and breaks on-site conversion tracking

Quality scores — the rebuilt site

Rubric scoring across 9 dimensions

Overall score: 5.6/10

Distinctiveness
Centered-headline + two-CTA hero is explicitly banned by the Must Not Do rules — this is the most disqualifying distinctiveness failure.
5
Typography
No visible text-wrap: balance on headlines — the hero headline breaks awkwardly at 'Experience,' creating an orphaned 'Digitally Perfected' line.
6
Color & contrast
Brass accent is completely absent from the design — the brief mandates it land exactly once on the CTA as the only warmth signal. This is both a color and distinctiveness failure.
6
Spatial & layout
Fidelity failure: the outer page background must be sage green (#D9DFD3) — it renders as off-white/cream. This collapses the entire three-tier depth system.
6
Information hierarchy
Two visually equivalent CTAs in the hero ('Schedule in Mansfield' and 'Explore Our Services') violate the single-primary-CTA-per-screen rule — one must be clearly subordinate.
7
UX writing
Service row descriptions are boilerplate: 'Restore damaged teeth with precisely fitted crowns that look and feel completely natural' — this sentence could appear on any dental site without editing. Must reference Dr. Lee's digital workflow or Mansfield specifically.
6
imagery
The doctor portrait placeholder ('L' on gray) is a hard imagery failure — it renders as an unresolved image state rather than an intentional design decision. This is a 404-equivalent broken image artifact.
3
Trust signals
Phone number is absent from the mobile header — the Must Do rules require a tel: link in the mobile header with minimum 44px touch target. Mobile screenshot shows logo + hamburger only. This is a hard rule violation.
5
