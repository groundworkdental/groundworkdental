Old site at a glance

A general-to-cosmetic dental practice that leads with technology as a brand name signal (Digital Dentistry) but does not clearly differentiate how that technology benefits patients beyond naming it

  • 1
    Zero patient testimonials or reviews surfaced
    this is a critical trust gap for a practice asking patients to invest in technology-forward care; Google review schema and on-site testimonial blocks must be added
  • 2
    No images found across crawled pages
    a digital dentistry practice with no visual content of the technology, office, or doctor completely undercuts the brand promise; before/after galleries, equipment photography, and a doctor headshot are essential
  • 3
    No phone number detected
    the most basic conversion element is missing or unscrapable, suggesting it may not be crawlable text (e.g., image-based or JavaScript-rendered), which is an accessibility and SEO failure
